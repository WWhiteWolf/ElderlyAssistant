WHAT'S NEXT
------------------------------------------------------------------
- THE REMINDERS — OPEN AGAIN (#14-new, #15-new, #16-new). Reading
  the whole of the reminder machinery found eight faults. Two are
  fixed and the third — the big one, the fault you reported — is
  fixed on one screen of three.
  The fault: a thing you tick off early still reminds you, because
  those three screens use the phone's own repeating alarm and a
  repeating alarm fires whether or not the item is done. The cure is
  to give each one a single moment aimed at the next time it is due,
  worked out fresh every time the app runs, so it can skip one you
  have already done. You agreed to that on one condition — that a
  missed firing is noted when you open the app — and that part is
  already built.
  What #16-new put in:
    Pets is cured. A feed you have already logged does not call out
      again that day, and still calls out the next day.
    Each feed is set two days ahead rather than repeating forever.
      Two was your call. The trade is real and worth knowing: if the
      app goes unopened longer than that, nothing new gets set and
      the reminder simply does not arrive. The pop-up tells you
      afterwards; it cannot make it arrive.
    Your places on the phone: the app can hold 56 reminders. At two
      days ahead, your fourteen items will use 28 of them, so there
      is room to spare. The Scheduled Reminders screen tells you how
      full it is, so you never have to take my word for the number.
    One test that had never been run since the very beginning now
      runs on the Mac: a day passing, an item ticked off, and
      whether it still reminds tomorrow.
    Two small things you may notice when this reaches the phone: a
      feed shows up twice on the Scheduled Reminders screen instead
      of once, because two days are set; and it says it does not
      repeat, which is now true.
  My Day is next and should go almost the same way. My Week goes
  last, because it clears its checkmarks differently from the other
  two and that difference is not settled yet.
  What #15-new put in:
    A pop-up when you open the app, if something went wrong. Until
      now the app could fail at reminders completely and look
      perfectly well. It speaks about the four things that mean a
      reminder will not arrive, and stays quiet about the two that
      do not. It comes back once a day rather than every time you
      come back to the app, so it does not become something you
      tap away without reading.
    A note of what never reached you, in the same pop-up. Written
      down as the day rolls over, since that is the last moment the
      app can still see what was left undone. One line per item,
      the most recent, in your Still To Do wording. Shown once and
      gone when you tap it. Being away a fortnight gives you a
      short list, not a wall.

  What was built, step by step:
    Step 1 (#6-new) — the new piece itself, plus its tests. No
      page touched.
    Step 2 (#7-new) — My Day and Pets stopped arming their own.
      This is the fix for the silence.
    Step 3 (#8-new) — My Week, Look Ahead and To-Do stopped too,
      and Orders came out of the reminding altogether.
    Step 4 — the snoozes, delays and postpones, in three pieces:
      My Week's postpone and Look Ahead's delay (#9-new), then My
      Day's and Pets' snoozes (#10-new), and then To-Do (#11-new),
      which needed nothing built. A To-Do banner has never had a
      Snooze button to tap, so the piece was taking out code that
      could never run.
    Step 5 (#11-new) — the Memory Test's five-minute recall.
    Step 6 (#11-new) — the warning when your phone can't hold
      every reminder asked for. It shows as you save the item,
      on the page you're on, and says the reminder furthest in
      the future won't go off until something makes room.
    Step 7 (#11-new) — the clean slate. Yesterday's checkmarks,
      snoozes and counts now clear whether or not you open My Day
      or Pets, and any banner from before today is taken down as

      means today now.
    Step 8 (#12-new) — Scheduled Reminders, in Settings under the
      three reminder times. It shows every reminder your phone is
      holding, under Today, Tomorrow, This Week and Later, with
      the soonest first. Tap any one and you get the whole story
      of it: what it is, which page it came from, when it fires

      next due, exactly what the banner will say, and which
      buttons it will carry. At the foot it tells you how full
      the phone is. Timer alerts are counted there but not
      listed, your call — the Timer is for short stretches and
      isn't something you'd look up.
      It cannot tell you whether a reminder actually went off.
      The phone keeps no record of that once a banner is
      dismissed, so there is nothing to read. What it does
      instead is show you when each one was DUE — so if a
      repeating reminder is missing from the list, that's the
      problem; if it's sitting there properly set and you heard
      nothing, the app did its part.
