  rings after you've said you're finished; and every routine
  screen now re-reads what's saved when you come back to it, so a

  line on the tile (your #61-build phone test). Reading the code

  already loaded never appears — and can even be lost. The Delay
  button ON the tile is fine. Fix is small (re-read on every visit).
  Also checked: a Delay is NOT supposed to write a log entry —
  only Done logs. That part is by design.
  #4-new: that fix is now in — Look Ahead re-reads on every visit.
  Very likely cured, but unconfirmed until the phone says so.
