FACTS THAT APPLY TO THE ONGOING WORK
------------------------------------------------------------------
Settled things worth having in front of you while the pages are
built. None of these is a job to do.

1. THE PAGES ARE NOT A REPLACEMENT FOR THE INPUT PAGE. The Input
   page goes away and nothing replaces it — and the + Add popup in
   particular is not its replacement. This is your own reminder
   pages being reshaped by how often a thing comes round.
2. + ADD ON A PAGE GOES TO THAT PAGE'S OWN NEW. Where does it
   belong? is only on Options, from + Screen. You do not set
   another page from a different page (#36-new).
3. APPOINTMENTS CARRIES THE REMINDERS BEFORE CHIPS — 30 min., 1
   hour, 2 hours, Morning of, Day Before, Night Before, 2 Days
   Before, Week, Month — and any and all of them can be on at once.
   They also remind at the set time, like the rest of the app
   (Patrick, #74-new; reverses #52-new). The page
   is called Appointments (#56-new; saved kind appointments, #65-new).
4. DAILY'S OWN ADD IS ONLY ABOUT TODAY. Two kinds: an every-day
   item, or a One Time for today. On One Time for today the
   Reminders before chips are only 30 min., 1 hour, 2 hours, and
   Time of (the item's own time). Save on that kind comes back on
   Appointments, which has the Log, not Daily. The other pages keep their
   own adds, because on those the page is the whole meaning of Repeat.
5. ONE REMINDER PER ITEM, NOT TWO, and the app puts itself right
   when you open it: it tells you what you missed, then sets the
   reminders again. That is your own reasoning and it replaced the
   old two-days-ahead rule. A Daily item still arms for tomorrow
   after Done; that is still one reminder, not two (Patrick, #44-new).
6. ROCK SOLID IS THE TOP GOAL BUT NOT THE ONLY ONE. Consistency
   stands beside it. That correction is yours, from #17-new.
7. ROCK SOLID IS FOR WHEN YOU USE IT. An app that is not being
   opened is not being used, and what the old second copy protected
   was one day and no more.
8. AN EXTENDED ITEM AND A DAILY TICK ARE OPPOSITES. A Bucket List
   item (saved kind bucketlist) says a thing is not yet done and must
   survive the night. The coffee-and-water sort says a thing was
   done today and is meaningless the morning after. They must never
   be merged.
9. TIMES SET BY SPINNING THROUGH NOON OR MIDNIGHT BEFORE #27-new
    are stored in the wrong half of the day and want re-setting.
10. TASKS SAVED BEFORE THE DATE FIX carry a date they were given
    rather than one you chose.
11. A BIG PIECE GETS A BUILD SHEET FIRST — self-contained, carrying
    every answer, so whoever builds it never has to ask you a
    design question.
12. BARE SESSION NUMBERS mean the old shared chain. This project's
    own chain is written #1-new, #2-new, and so on.
13. WORKING DOCUMENTS LIVE IN docs. History lives in docs-ref, and
    the build sheets are in docs-ref/build-sheets. The index
    says which file is the home. He calls them working documents;
    live desk means the same thing. (App-Docs, 2026-08-31)
14. THE CALENDAR PAGE SHOWS DATED ITEMS. No every-day Daily items,
    no Bucket List. No Done or Snooze on the month or the day’s list.
    Names in a day box are one line and scroll vertically.
    Landscape is an optional view: 0°, 90° counter-clockwise, and
    270° counter-clockwise; 180° upside-down is out. Headers stay
    at the physical top of the phone, all pages. The calendar month
    grid was not rebuilt.
15. HAVING A GOOD IDEA LEADS TO OVERREACHING (Patrick, #52-new).
    The feedback he needs is the trim: what part is worth it,
    and what is more than is there.

   The run record on Scheduled Reminders. The last run is already
   written down, and a failure already speaks in the opening
   pop-up. That pop-up can be tapped away, and it only talks
   about the newest run. Scheduled Reminders only shows what the
   phone is holding now. This would put the record on that screen
   so you can look later at whether the last run went well or
   failed, including the quieter faults the pop-up never raises.
   Held back at #15-new and never built.

   Check My Reminders. The pop-up knows when the app itself
   failed, or when permission is off. It does not know why a
   banner still never appeared: permission granted quietly,
   alerts or sound or the lock screen off, Focus, a scheduled
   summary. A Settings row you tap on purpose: it checks those
   things, and it can send one test reminder so you can see
   whether the phone will actually speak. Never built.

   A background task to top the queue. This is not a check. It
   tries to keep the queue topped up on days the app is not
   opened. It does not explain a silence. None of it is
   installed. The phone gives it least to an unused app, and none
   if the app has been swiped away, so it can only ever sit on
   top of arming ahead, never underneath.
