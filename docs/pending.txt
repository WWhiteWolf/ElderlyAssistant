  item, and this has never actually been tried with a real tap.

PARKED ON PURPOSE (bigger items, on hold for now)
-------------------------------------------------
- Voice control through Siri. It gets you to the app and tapping works, but
  Siri won't recognize spoken item names ("mark snack done"). After a lot of
  effort this was set aside. Revisit if a future iPhone update fixes it.
- The Timer "Loud alert" isn't actually louder — it's the normal alert tone.
  A genuinely louder, distinct alarm needs a custom sound file built into the
  app (a full rebuild), and breaking through Silent / Do-Not-Disturb needs a
  special Apple permission meant for medical/safety apps. Heavy — deferred.
- A running Timer's tile disappears if the app reloads or restarts (the list
  of active timers is only held in memory). Accepted as-is: tapping "Done"
  still stops the alerts whether or not the tile is showing.
