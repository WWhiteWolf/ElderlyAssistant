## Load by Restore, not by hand

There is a backup file at `docs/Remember-Backup-Sit-near-fire.json`.
Restore **replaces everything** in Memory: your lists, your name,
your theme, the Vault. It cannot merge.

1. On the simulator or the phone, Memory, Backup & Restore, **Export
   Backup** first. Keep that file. That is how you get your real list
   back.
2. Copy `Remember-Backup-Sit-near-fire.json` onto that device
   (Files, or share from the Mac).
3. Backup & Restore, **Import Backup**, pick that Sit file, Replace.
4. When the sitting is done, Import your own export from step 1.

The Sit file is this week only: Tuesday 1 September 2026. The last
rolled date in it is 1 September, so Wednesday morning is a new day.
